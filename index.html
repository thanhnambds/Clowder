Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose an automatic litter box?

Start with your cat. Compare your cat's shoulder width with the box's opening and your cat's weight with its limit. Then look at bin life, noise and price.

Do automatic litter boxes work for big cats?

Some do. The three models we compare list weight limits of 22, 30 and 33 lbs, and openings from 10.51 x 10.74 in up to 15.75 x 15.75 in. Our large cats guide goes through the differences.

Is an automatic litter box worth the money?

It depends on how much you dislike scooping and whether your cat will accept one. The models here run from $379.99 to $799. Our worth-it guide breaks down the costs.
