Good for: big cats, cats that dislike enclosed boxes, shoppers watching the budget.

Think twice if: noise matters in your home, or you want an enclosed box that hides the litter.

What Neakasa says

The entry height is 13.86 inches. Neakasa doesn't list the width or depth. It rates the box for cats from 2.2 to 33 lbs, which is the widest range of the models here. The bin holds 11.23 L and lasts about 14 days for one cat. It takes waste liners, so check Neakasa's guidance on which to buy.

For safety, Neakasa lists 6 sets of sweeping infrared sensors plus 4 weight sensors. It says the M1 Plus adds a built-in sealing strip and a custom-fit sealing layer between the litter chamber and the base, which it says reduces urine leaks.

Noise is 50 dB. Empty, the unit weighs 22.81 lbs. Neakasa doesn't list the outside dimensions.

Warranty: one year (limited) in the US and Canada, two years in the EU and UK.

How it compares

Next to the Litter-Robot 5 ($799, Check on Amazon), it costs less than half as much and takes bigger cats. The trade-offs are noise and the lack of an enclosure. Questions

What's the difference between an open-top box and a globe?

An open basin has no roof and no door, so tall or long cats have less to squeeze through. An enclosed globe hides the litter and can contain more odor and mess.

What liners does it use?

It uses waste liners for the 11.23 L bin. Check Neakasa's documentation for specifics.
