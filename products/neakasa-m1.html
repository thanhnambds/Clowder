ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN

Open-Top Basin Architecture

The Neakasa M1 Plus features an open-top sifting basin format rather than an enclosed rotating drum.

  • • Entry step height is published at 13.86 inches (width and depth not stated).
  • • Stated cat weight limits accommodate pets from 2.2 to 33 lbs.
  • • Open basin format without an enclosed entryway tunnel.

Questions & Answers About Neakasa M1 Plus

What are the design differences between an open-top automatic box and an enclosed globe?

The Neakasa M1 Plus features an open-top sifting basin format with a 13.86" entry height, whereas globe models feature an enclosed rotating drum with an entryway portal.

What waste bags does the Neakasa M1 Plus use?

The M1 uses waste liners for its 11.23L collection bin. Check manufacturer documentation for liner specifications.

What safety sensors are specified for the Neakasa M1 Plus?

The manufacturer specifies 6 sets of sweeping infrared sensors + 4 weight sensors. Neakasa states the M1 Plus adds a built-in sealing strip and a custom-fit sealing layer between the litter chamber and the base, which it says reduces urine leaks.
