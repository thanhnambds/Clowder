ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN

Open-Top Basin Architecture

The Neakasa M1 features an open-top sifting basin format rather than an enclosed rotating drum.

  • • Entry step height is published at 13.86 inches (width and depth not stated).
  • • Stated cat weight limits accommodate pets from 2.2 to 33 lbs.
  • • Open basin format without an enclosed entryway tunnel.

WASTE CONTAINER & NOISE

Waste Capacity & Sound Level

The unit features a dedicated waste collection bin located beneath the open sifting basin.

  • • 11.23L waste container capacity stated for about 14 days for one cat.

Questions & Answers About Neakasa M1

What are the design differences between an open-top automatic box and an enclosed globe?

The Neakasa M1 features an open-top sifting basin format with a 13.86" entry height, whereas globe models feature an enclosed rotating drum with an entryway portal.

What waste bags does the Neakasa M1 use?

The M1 uses waste liners for its 11.23L collection bin. Check manufacturer documentation for liner specifications.
