Good for: cats within the 3.3 to 22 lb range whose owners want a built-in camera and a 13 L waste drawer.

Think twice if: your cat is near or above 22 lb, or you have very small kittens under 3.3 lb.

Size at a glance

Who the numbers suit

Based only on the maker's figures: a 13 in x 10.6 in entrance and a stated limit of 22 lb mean cats near or above that weight are outside the stated range. The 3.3 lb minimum rules out very small kittens. This is arithmetic on the published specifications, with no promises about fit or comfort.

What the maker does not say

CATLINK does not state a minimum cat age, supported litter types, or the unit's power rating. In addition, the maker's page gives two different minimum weights: 3.3 lb in the Specs section and 3 lb 8 oz in the Product Details section. Check with CATLINK before buying if you need those details.

The catch

The camera and Wi-Fi features depend on the CATLINK app, which we have not used. The 1-year warranty is what the maker lists; read its terms before you buy.
