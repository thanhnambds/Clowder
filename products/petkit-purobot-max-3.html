Good for: smaller or older cats that do better with a low step, and a quiet, mid-priced box.

Think twice if: you have several cats (the bin is the smallest here) or a cat near the 22 lb limit.

What PETKIT says

The entrance is 10.51 x 10.74 inches, and the entrance height is 8.03 inches. PETKIT rates the box for cats from 3.3 to 22 lbs. The 7 L bin lasts up to 15 days for one cat.

For safety, PETKIT lists 12 sensors plus an anti-pinch structure that it says keeps paws from getting trapped as the cylinder turns. Noise is 35 dB. The unit measures 24.4 x 21.2 x 21.7 in. Warranty is two years under PETKIT's warranty policy for US purchases from PETKIT.com or authorized retailers.

How it compares

It costs $100 less than the Max Pro 2 ($499.99) and has the same weight limit, sensor count and noise figure. The bin is smaller (7 L vs. 8 L) and the Max Pro 2 adds an AI camera. See the full comparison.

Questions

Why choose a lower entrance?

A lower step can be easier for small, young or older cats to climb. PETKIT lists 8.03 inches. Check the weight minimum (3.3 lbs) for kittens.
