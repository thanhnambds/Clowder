Good for: big cats, homes with up to five cats, anyone who wants the roomiest enclosed opening.

Think twice if: you're on a budget, short on floor space (22 x 27 in), or want a published noise figure.

What Whisker says

The opening is 15.75 x 15.75 inches. Whisker rates the box for cats from 3 to 30 lbs and up to five cats. Waste goes into a sealed drawer beneath the globe, and Whisker says it lasts up to 10 days for one cat. It doesn't give the drawer's volume.

For safety, Whisker lists multiple lasers plus a weight sensor, and classes it as a Class 1 laser product. It doesn't publish a decibel figure. It says its QuietSift technology keeps noise down.

The box measures 22 x 27 in at the base and stands 29.9 in tall. Empty, it weighs 28.7 lbs.

Warranty: one year included, with an optional three-year plan. The plan's price varies.

How it compares

Against the Neakasa M1 Plus ($379.99) and the PETKIT Max Pro 2 ($499.99), the Litter-Robot 5 has the largest opening and is the only one that states a cat count. It also costs the most. See the full comparison.

Questions

How many cats can use it?

Whisker says up to five, each between 3 and 30 lbs.

Is it quiet?

Whisker doesn't list a decibel figure, so we can't say. It says QuietSift technology keeps noise down.
