Our Research & Evaluation Methodology

How we aggregate manufacturer data, analyze technical specifications, and present comparative guides for smart pet appliances.

Important Clarification: We do not test products ourselves. We are not an engineering testing laboratory. Our comparisons and tables are compiled from published manufacturer technical datasheets, user manuals, and dimension sheets.

Our guides are published under the byline Sam Ellery, the pen name of the ClowderHub research desk.

1. Product Selection & Ranking Criteria

Products are selected and ordered based strictly on their functional suitability for specific feline and household needs—such as multi-cat durability, large-breed clearance, or tight apartment spaces—never on commercial affiliate potential:

  • Need-Based Organization: Models are categorized by household configuration and design (e.g. multi-cat capacities, open-top basins, and rotating globes).
  • Reference Models: We include a widely known baseline model (the Litter-Robot 5) so readers can compare against it. It is marked Reference Model. Links to it are partner links and may earn us a commission, like the other products.
  • Commercial Separation: Affiliate commissions do not dictate ranking position or inclusion.

2. The 4 Technical Specification Areas We Track

Area A: Anti-Pinch & Safety Sensors

We examine manufacturer-stated safety systems: multi-zone infrared optical arrays, base weight load cells, and automatic motor torque cutoffs designed to pause drum rotation when a cat is detected.

Area B: Usable Dimensions & Entry Form Factor

We compile stated entry portal diameter, step height from the floor, and interior basin volume. We categorize appliances by form factor: enclosed rotating globe, open-top basin, or mechanical rake.

Area C: Waste Bin Capacity & Multi-Cat Practicality

Manufacturer claims of "14 days hands-free" typically specify a single small cat. We compare stated waste container volumes to assist multi-cat owners in evaluating disposal intervals.

Area D: Odor Sealing & Hygiene Engineering

We evaluate stated odor containment mechanisms: sealed drawers and waste bin designs.
