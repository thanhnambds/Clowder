About ClowderHub

Providing clear, specification-based comparisons of automatic, self-cleaning, and smart cat litter boxes for cat parents and multi-pet homes.

Important Clarification: We do not test products ourselves. We are an editorial aggregation and research publication. Our guides are compiled from published manufacturer specifications and dimension schematics.

Our guides are published under the byline Sam Ellery, the pen name of the ClowderHub research desk.

Our Mission

The smart pet technology market encompasses a variety of automatic litter boxes with different designs. For cat owners—including households with multiple cats or large breeds (15+ lbs)—finding clear manufacturer specifications on entry dimensions, weight limits, and waste capacity is essential.

ClowderHub compiles published manufacturer technical datasheets, entry dimensions, anti-pinch safety sensors, and waste bin volumes to provide clean, side-by-side comparisons.

What We Focus On

  • Anti-Pinch & Sensor Safety: Checking whether units employ multi-zone optical curtains, weight load cells, and automatic motor cut-offs designed to protect pets from entrapment.
  • Multi-Cat Waste Capacity: Evaluating stated waste drawer volumes so multi-cat households aren't forced to empty trays every 48 hours.
  • Entry Ergonomics & Form Factor: Comparing open-top basins (like Neakasa M1 Plus) against rotating globes (like Litter-Robot 5) across published entry opening dimensions and cat weight limits.
  • Odor Control Mechanisms: Analyzing sealing gaskets, and waste drawer mechanisms.

Editorial Standards & Selection Criteria

Products featured in our guides are selected and ordered based on their functional suitability for specific feline needs (such as multi-cat homes, large breeds, or budget constraints), never by commercial affiliate partnerships. We include widely known reference models alongside partner links to ensure balanced comparisons.
