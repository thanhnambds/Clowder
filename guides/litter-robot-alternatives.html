What you give up, and what you gain

Neakasa M1 Plus. You save over $400 against the Litter-Robot 5's starting price and get a larger bin (11.23 L) and a higher weight limit (33 lbs). You trade away quiet (50 dB) and an enclosed design. Neakasa doesn't list the entry width and depth.

PETKIT Purobot Max Pro 2. You save about $300 and get the quietest listed figure (35 dB). You trade away room: the opening is smaller and the weight limit drops to 22 lbs. PETKIT's warranty policy lists two years for US purchases from PETKIT.com or authorized retailers.
