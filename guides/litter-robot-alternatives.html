1. Why Consider an Alternative?

Shoppers evaluate alternatives to the flagship rotating globe across three primary factors:

01. Form Factor Preference: Open-top basin format vs enclosed rotating cylinder or globe.
02. Waste Handling Capacity: Comparing waste bin volumes and stated change intervals.
03. Price Tiers: Comparing manufacturer sale pricing across form factors.
