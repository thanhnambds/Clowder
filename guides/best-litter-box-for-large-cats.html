1. Entry Dimensions & Interior Clearance Considerations

Entry dimensions and interior clearance determine how larger cats enter and comfortably use an automatic appliance:

01

Open-Top vs Enclosed Domes

Open-top basin units remove vertical clearance ceilings entirely, making entry natural for taller or long-bodied breeds that feel boxed in.

02

Entry Opening & Diagonal Fit

Enclosed spheres specify fixed portals (from 10.51" x 10.74" to 15.75" x 15.75"). Compare against your cat's shoulder and chest width.

03

Weight Limit Calibration

Units calibrate sensors up to 22, 30, or 33 lbs. Ensure the appliance weight threshold comfortably exceeds your pet's current adult weight.

04

Interior Turning Radius

Larger felines require sufficient circular base clearance to circle, dig, and squat without pressing against motorized sifting ribs.
