1. Entry Dimensions & Interior Clearance Considerations

Entry dimensions and interior clearance determine how larger cats enter an automatic appliance:

- Enclosed rotating globes and cylinders specify fixed portal dimensions (ranging from 10.51" x 10.74" to 15.75" x 15.75").
- Open-top sifting basin designs feature an open upper perimeter with published entry step heights (such as 13.86").
